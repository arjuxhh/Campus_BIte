Dish photos
-----------
Put one photo per dish in this folder, named after the dish in lower case with dashes:

  "Idli & Sambar"              -> idli-sambar.jpg
  "Malabar Chicken Biriyani"   -> malabar-chicken-biriyani.jpg
  "Kerala Meals"               -> kerala-meals.jpg
  "Kerala Porotta & Curry"     -> kerala-porotta-curry.jpg
  "Chicken Cutlet"             -> chicken-cutlet.jpg
  "Egg Puffs"                  -> egg-puffs.jpg
  "Pazham Pori"                -> pazham-pori.jpg
  "Parippu Vada"               -> parippu-vada.jpg
  "Filter Coffee"              -> filter-coffee.jpg

Photos are centre-cropped to fit the menu cards, never stretched. Dishes without a photo show a coloured tile with their icon.
Admin-added dishes can also pick a photo from disk in the Add menu item dialog.
